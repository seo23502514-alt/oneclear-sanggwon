Samsung CM1919
Microwave Cook, reheat and defrost a wide variety of foods using one of the most popular professional microwaves on the market - the manual Samsung 1850W commercial microwave. With its impressive reputation for reliability, performance and efficiency, chefs and cooks around the world have long used the Samsung to save time and effort, whilst still getting great cooking results every time.
With simple manual controls, the Samsung couldn't be easier to use - just set the time and power level and then close the door. Cleaning is incredibly easy too, with its stainless steel construction and time-saving removable roof liner - especially useful when dishes aren't quite covered correctly. For further flexibility, the Samsung is big enough to fit microwave-safe 2/3GN size containers, so there's plenty of performance to microwave large quantities all at the same time. Whether it's installed in a café, restaurant or canteen, the Samsung microwave is a great choice for the professional.
