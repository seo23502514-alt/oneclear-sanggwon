Frequently Asked Questions
How many cups per day can the Saeco SE180 handle?
The SE180 is rated for 80–120 cups per hour, making it suitable for large offices, co-working spaces and medium-to-large hospitality venues. In high-use environments this translates to sustained capacity well above 500 cups per day. It's purpose-built for commercial volume — not a domestic machine adapted for office use.
What does IntelliGrind technology do?
IntelliGrind automatically maintains a consistent coffee dose regardless of bean type, roast level or grinder wear. Without it, automatic machines need periodic manual adjustment as burrs wear, causing dose inconsistency and flavour variation. IntelliGrind eliminates this, ensuring every cup is extracted to the same parameters with no manual intervention from staff.
Why is the refrigerated milk fridge important?
Room-temperature milk produces inconsistent microfoam and begins to degrade in quality and food safety within hours of being set out. The Precision refrigerated fridge connects directly to the SE180's milk circuit, holding milk at the precise chilled temperature for consistent frothing — so the latte at 3pm matches the one at 9am, with full food-safety compliance throughout the day.
Can the SE180 be plumbed into a water line?
Yes. The SE180 supports both internal tank operation (8-litre reservoir) and direct plumb-in to a building water supply. Plumb-in is strongly recommended for high-volume environments — it eliminates tank management and ensures uninterrupted service through peak periods. Our installation team handles the plumbing connection as part of free Sydney metro installation.
