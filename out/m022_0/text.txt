ESPRESSO MACHINE
STUDIO
BE YOUR OWN BARISTA
Compact, accurate, efficient. The new Dalla Corte Studio is an espresso machine with a professional soul and household attitude: it fits perfectly at home, at the office or in small shops. Discover the new Studio: the pleasure of making the perfect espresso.
Features
Small and elegant, designed to fit even the smallest of places, Studio is the ideal choice for houses, offices, restaurants and bakeries. It can be used via water supply or tank.
The Studio has been equipped with the same steam wand on all Dalla Corte professional machines: a powerful tool able to guarantee optimal frothing.
Thermal stability and performance consistency make the Studio an ideal espresso machine for professional experimentation
The coffee machine that is able to adapt to your personality, your space and style of living. Choose the colour that matches your style, the Studio’s finishes available in black or white.
The digital display controls all parameters; on/off timer and automatic cleaning: just some of the new functions of the DC Studio that can help you become a great barista
Be your own barista: The Studio, Dalla Corte’s professional one-group home espresso machine, features an elegant design and highly technological parts.
