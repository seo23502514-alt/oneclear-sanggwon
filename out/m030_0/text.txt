Specifications
U240-Spec-Sheet
Product Description
Keep ice within easy reach during drinks service with the Manitowoc NEO U240 cube ice maker. Producing up to 87kg per day with a built-in 36kg storage bin, it suits restaurants, bars, cafés and delicatessens that need daily ice production and a reserve for busy periods.
Available in full dice or half dice configurations, the U240 lets you choose the cube style that suits your drinks menu. Front-mounted controls make operation straightforward, while production delay settings help you plan around quieter hours. Tool-free foodzone access simplifies regular cleaning, and a 3-year parts and labour warranty provides added confidence in your purchase.
Key Features
Up to 87kg daily production at 21°C air and 10°C water.
Integrated 36kg bin combines ice making and storage without a separate bin.
Full dice or half dice options to suit your beverage service.
Accessible front keypad keeps operating controls within easy reach.
4, 12 or 24-hour production delay allows you to pause ice making around your schedule.
Easy Clean technology and tool-free foodzone access make routine cleaning more convenient.
Corrosion- and fingerprint-resistant composite exterior helps maintain a tidy appearance.
Modular design supports straightforward servicing.
10A plug for electrical connection.
150mm legs supplied, with an optional set of four 30mm feet available.
Optional LuminIce® II Growth Inhibitor available to complement regular cleaning and sanitising.
Manufacturer’s warranty: 3 years parts and labour.
