Product Features
98 mm Steel K110 Burrs
98 mm blind burrs made from high-strength Steel K110 improve particle distribution and reduce waste, with a burr life rated up to 9,000 kg. Larger, durable burrs keep extraction consistent as you refine recipes over time.
Micrometric Regulation
Micrometric regulation system gives you real, repeatable control over grind size with a TRUE PRECISION micrometer reading from 0 to 900 microns. That level of adjustment helps prosumers precisely dial extraction across espresso and filter recipes.
Low-Speed Cold Grinding
On 110 V / 60 Hz power, the PK100 uses a 960 W motor at 1,100 RPM. Compak specifies a maximum cycle of 1 minute grinding followed by 3 minutes resting.
Single Dose System
The Lab configuration uses a 55 ± 5 g single-dose cup instead of the Shop model’s bean hopper. Its low-retention path suits cupping, recipe work, and frequent coffee changes.
Adjustable Exit & Hands-Free Fork
Height-adjustable exit chute and a ‘hands-free’ universal fork accommodate portafilters, dosing cups, and beakers to speed workflow and reduce fuss. Optional Espresso/Filter/Cupping forks let you tailor the setup to your preferred service style.
Shop-Ready Configuration

Dual Fan Ventilation
A dual fan cooling system is an essential component of coffee grinders, as it plays
a vital role in maintaining performance and preserving coffee quality
. Here's why it's important:
Heat Reduction:
Coffee grinding creates heat through the friction between the burrs and beans, which can negatively impact the flavor and aroma of the coffee. The dual fan system actively dissipates this heat to prevent the grounds from overheating during grinding.
Consistent Extraction:
Heat can affect the extraction process, causing premature release of volatile compounds from hot coffee grounds and leading to uneven extraction. The dual fan system maintains a consistent temperature to ensure balanced flavors in espresso shots.
Shot Time Stability:
In busy cafes, consistent shot times are crucial, but heat buildup can cause variations in grind particle size and extraction times. The dual fan cooling system minimizes these fluctuations, allowing baristas to maintain precise shot times even during peak hours.

Product FAQ
What types of coffee preparation is the Compak PK100 Lab Coffee Grinder suitable for (espresso, filter, cupping, etc.)?
The Compak PK100 Lab is designed as a highly precise, multi‑purpose grinder. It can be dialed in for espresso, pour‑over, batch brew, Aeropress, French press, and cupping. Its fine adjustments and low‑retention design make it especially popular as a single‑dose grinder for both espresso and filter brewing in cafés and at home.
Is the Compak PK100 Lab a stepless grinder, and how easy is it to adjust the grind size?
Yes. The PK100 Lab uses a stepless adjustment system, allowing infinitely fine changes within its range rather than fixed ‘clicks.’ This makes it very precise for dialing in espresso while still allowing quick shifts to coarser filter settings. The adjustment collar is clearly marked and designed for smooth, repeatable changes.
Does the PK100 Lab work as a single‑dose grinder, and how much coffee retention does it have?
The PK100 Lab is engineered for single‑dose use, with a bellows system and vertical burr orientation that help minimize retention. Under typical use, retention is very low (often well under a gram), which is why it’s favored for workflow that involves weighing each dose in and out for consistency and minimal waste.
How fast does the Compak PK100 Lab grind, and can it keep up with multiple drinks in a row?
Compak publishes approximately 5.5 g/second at espresso settings and 10 g/second at filter settings. That equals roughly 350 g/minute espresso or 600 g/minute filter under test conditions; no daily maximum is published.
What type and size of burrs does the Compak PK100 Lab Coffee Grinder use?
The PK100 Lab is equipped with large, vertically mounted flat burrs (around 98 mm, depending on the specific variant). These burrs are designed to produce a very uniform particle distribution with excellent clarity for both espresso and filter coffee. Different burr options or finishes may be available depending on configuration.
How long do the burrs in the PK100 Lab typically last, and how often should they be replaced?
Burr life depends on roast level, volume, and use (espresso vs. filter), but large flat burrs in this class often last several thousand pounds of coffee before performance noticeably declines. In a busy café, that might mean replacement every 1–2 years; in a low‑volume shop or home environment, they can last many years. Signs it’s time to replace burrs include slower grinding, more fines, and difficulty achieving consistent shots at your usual settings.
Will the Compak PK100 Lab fit under most kitchen cabinets in the USA?
The PK100 Lab is relatively tall for a home kitchen and may not fit under standard 18-inch (about 46 cm) upper cabinets once the hopper or single‑dose funnel is installed. Many users place it at the front edge of the counter or in an area without overhead cabinetry. Check the grinder’s published height and compare it to your measured clearance before purchasing.
Does the PK100 Lab offer hands‑free operation or grind‑by‑weight and timed dosing features?
The PK100 Lab is primarily designed for single‑dosing and manual workflow. It does not include an integrated grind‑by‑weight system. Depending on the configuration, it may include timed grinding controls or be operated manually. For precise dosing, most users weigh their beans before grinding and use an external scale to confirm dose output.

What to Expect on Install Day
Getting a commercial espresso machine delivered is one thing. Getting it actually running — plumbed, programmed, and pulling properly — is another. Here's what happens when the technician comes out.
Before the appointment:
Site preparation will be confirmed before technicians make it out. Once your machine ships, we'll coordinate scheduling with you and assign a local certified technician. Before they arrive, make sure your space is ready: the machine's permanent location should be determined, your water line and drain access should be in place, and your electrical supply should match the machine's requirements.
On the day
The technician will handle the physical installation of the machine — positioning, water line connection, and drain setup. Once it's plumbed and powered, they'll run through the setup and basic programming: boiler pressure, shot parameters, and confirming that water flows correctly through the group heads and steam wands. Some basic training will be provided, they'll pull test shots to verify the machine is operating within spec.
After Installation
Once your machine is installed, we'll provide referrals for authorized service technicians in your area for ongoing maintenance and repairs. Coffeeionado offers lifetime support on all equipment we sell — we're here long after the install is done.
