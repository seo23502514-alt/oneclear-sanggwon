Bolt 64
Instant speed. Everyday versatility.
BOLT 64 brings the speed of
Instant Dose
technology to a versatile professional espresso grinder. The next dose is kept ready during busy service, allowing the barista to keep the workflow moving without waiting for the grinder.
Its dual Pre-Dose and On Demand operation adapts naturally to changing service volumes, while 64 mm flat burrs deliver consistent grinding performance throughout the day.
