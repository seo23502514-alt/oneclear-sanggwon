Description
Fetco E213157 efficiently brews hot delicious coffee up to a capacity of 9.7 gallons per hour. Coffee shops, banquet halls and cafeterias can rely on the consistent production of this coffee maker. Designed to add to the convenience of the user, this automatic coffee brewer has touchscreen display that offers access to all the controls at one location. It offers instant feedbacks of the programming, diagnostics and the brew cycles.
This electric coffee maker has a manually operated faucet and sensors that stop the hot water cycle when the brew baskets are removed. Fetco coffee brewer is easy to clean and maintain. It has no small drain holes for water flow, the water runs on the outer surface. The cascading spray dome cannot be clogged due to lime build-up. The stainless-steel exteriors remain rust free for longer and can be trusted for longer serviceable life.
This single coffee maker has batch-to-batch and brewer-to-brewer copying functionality. You can copy the batch parameters from one coffee machine and transfer the same to a USB device. The copied parameters can now be installed on multiple machines. This coffee making machine has an output of 2200-3100W and it requires 240V electric connection.
