Product Description
Scotsman Gourmet ACS 86 AS Ice Maker
The Scotman Gourmet AC 86 Ice Maker is a top-of-the-line commercial ice machine, perfect for any home or business. With its stylish design and advanced features, this ice maker offers fast and reliable ice-making performance. It's easy to use and produces up to 86 lbs of ice per day, making it ideal for large events and occasions.
Its self-cleaning cycle helps to keep the machine hygienic and running smoothly, and its adjustable settings allow you to customize the ice size and shape to suit your needs. Get the perfect ice every time with the Scotman Gourmet AC 86 Ice Maker.
