A robust home coffee machine, the Wega W Mini EMA features a reliable group head, hot water wand, and a steam wand to cover all of your coffee making needs. It also comes with a full suite of accessories which include a milk jug, tamper, portafilter, filter baskets, and cleaning implements to keep your machine in top shape.
Inside, the Wega W Mini EMA has a 2.5-litre water tank and 1.8-litre boiler capacity which makes it more than capable when it’s time to serve coffee to your guests. At 425mm x 360mm, this coffee machine fits beautifully into any space without hogging too much kitchen real estate.
Wega W Mini Coffee Machine Features
2.5-litre water tank -
Created for home use, the Wega W Mini’s 2.5-litre tank keeps refills to a minimum.
Complete accessories -
This Wega coffee machine comes with a complete set of coffee making accessories so you can start practising pulling your shots right out of the box.
Hot water wand -
In addition to the steam wand, the Wega W Mini is also equipped with a hot water wand for your other beverage needs.
Durable -
Its stainless steel body and reliable components ensure longevity and minimal maintenance efforts.
This product includes:
1x Wega Mini EMA Coffee Machine
2x Portafilters (Single and Double Spouted)
2x Filter Baskets (8g & 16g)
1x Metal Coffee Tamper
1x Wega 0.5L Milk Jug
1x Blind Filter Basket
1x Microfibre Cloth
Cleaning Brush & Cleaning Tablets

Freight Protection
We strive to ensure that your delivery arrives safely to you, but we understand that sometimes accidents happen outside of our control.
By selecting Alternative Brewing's Freight Protection you will receive a priority customer support and priority replacement of your product if it arrives damaged. You will be able to add this option to your order in checkout.
What does Freight Protection offer?
1 - Priority Customer Service – as soon as you contact our team to submit your Freight Protection claim, you will be placed into a priority queue to ensure your claim is processed as quickly as possible.
2 - Advanced Shipping – to minimise downtime without your product, once we have assessed your claim we will ship a replacement to you before we have received the damaged goods back.
Making a Freight Protection Claim
To assess your claim, you will be required to provide photo and/or video evidence of the damaged product.
All Freight Protections claims must be made within 3 business days of receiving your product.
Freight Protection does not apply to damaged packaging and only applies to the product itself.
Pre Order Shipping
Orders that contain pre-order items will be placed on hold until all products are in stock and ready to ship together. This helps ensure your order arrives complete in a single delivery.
If you would like the in-stock items from your order shipped sooner, we can arrange a split shipment upon request. Please note that an additional delivery fee will apply for this service.
Estimated shipping times listed on our website only apply once your full order has been dispatched. Pre-order release dates are provided as a guide by the manufacturer and may be subject to change. While these timeframes are outside of our control, we will always do our best to keep you informed and update you as soon as we receive any new information regarding availability.
If your order contains both pre-order and in-stock items and you choose to wait for a complete delivery, your order will be dispatched in full as soon as the pre-order stock becomes available.
