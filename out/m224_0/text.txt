MEIKO M-iQ FLIGHT TYPE DISHWASHER
The sweet spot of hygiene and efficiency with MEIKO.
Technical Data
PROFESSIONAL
DISHWASHER
MEIKO M-iQ B series
If you are looking for incredible cleaning, and efficient use of water and cleaning agents without having to compromise on energy efficiency, then the M-iQ is the right flight type dishwasher for you. All of this is made possible by a range of innovations that have revolutionized the warewashing process – and they all come together to benefit you. You see, hygienic warewashing does not have to come down to more water and energy – we just need to be smarter.

M-iQ FEATURES
for cleaning, economy, and optimum resource efficiency

M-iQ ENERGY CONCEPT
M-iQ ENERGY CONCEPT
Heat is recovered and retained within the warewashing process at every stage. Any further heating performance that is required – and not a degree more – is fed back into the process using our three-stage management system to dynamically adapt to the needs of the moment.
Advantages:
Clever energy distribution to get the balance just right
Up to 30% lower operating costs
2
M-iQ AIRCOMFORT DRYING SYSTEM
M-iQ AIRCOMFORT DRYING SYSTEM
Equalization vents separate the airflows used in the M-iQ’s drying process from the dishwashing area. This delivers excellent drying results while valuable energy is retained within the warewashing process at every stage of operation. It also provides fantastic convenience at the unloading workstation.
Advantages:
Perfect drying results – on plates, cups, and containers
Exceptionally convenient to unload
3
M-iQ WASHING DYNAMICS
M-iQ WASHING DYNAMICS
The power center of the M-iQ is the self-emptying stainless steel wash pump. Its optimized flow geometry achieves high pressure with low volumes.
Advantages:
30% better washing performance (as compared to the previous machine line, the B-Tronic)
Maximum cleaning power using 1/3 less resources and energy
4
M-iQ INFEED SECTION CLEANING
M-iQ INFEED SECTION CLEANING
Hygienic working starts right from the feeding section: Food waste is automatically washed off at regular intervals using a powerful blast of water. Just like a big, cleaning waterfall!
5
M-iQ AIRCONCEPT
M-iQ AIRCONCEPT
The air is channeled from the hot, clean side of the machine to the cool feeding zone. This prevents dishes which have already been washed from becoming contaminated and ensures that precious thermal energy remains in the warewashing process.
Advantages:
Great energy efficiency and hygiene safety
Standard-setting exhaust air measures mean no need to install a cost-intensive exhaust air system
Pleasant indoor climate
6
M-iQ FILTER
M-iQ FILTER
Food waste is filtered out of the water and then actively removed from the tank with no additional water – completely eliminating it from the warewashing process.
Advantages:
The wash water is more effective
Significantly lower dishwashing detergent and water consumption
Save money with lower operating costs
M-iQ AIRCOMFORT DRYING SYSTEM
It’s not all about the immense cleaning performance. The drying results are also astounding. Equalization vents inside the M-iQ ensure that the swirling airflows are equalized by extra draughts to keep them constant. The result: ideal conditions and top-notch drying even with bulky washware like containers.
M-iQ AIRSTREAM TECHNOLOGY
In addition to the equalization vents (M-iQ AirComfort drying), four targeted airflows keep the energy within the fully closed system air cycle. A moving nozzle brings the airflow as close as possible to the washware for 360 degree drying.
RESOURCE EFFICIENT
with the M-iQ filter
The filter system is a highlight of the M-iQ. It does an amazing job, delivering top-notch cleanliness while significantly reducing the use of water and dishwashing detergent.
But what’s so important about it? Filtered food waste is actively removed from the warewashing process. So how does it work? The filter collects food residue from the water and periodically washes it out of the tank – without requiring any additional water.
OPTIMUM EFFECTIVENESS
with M-iQ tank management
The wash water is continually transferred from tank to tank – in the opposite direction to the dishes. On its way, it passes through the various M-iQ filters, which remove any dirt that has accumulated in the water.
Advantages:
Efficient use of resources
Residual dirt is collected as far away as possible from the clean side
The M-iQ filters also monitor the water level in each tank

LEADING THE WAY
with M-iQ GreenEye technology® (optional)
The light guides the way: M-iQ
GreenEye
technology® not only identifies gaps and adjust its performance accordingly, it also evaluates those gaps for the machine operator. This actively assists the warewashing staff in operating the dishwasher at maximum efficiency. The machine’s specific recommendations are clearly visible at the feed point in the form of a green light which shows users how to achieve optimum belt and line capacity utilization.
ALWAYS ON THE RIGHT TRACK
with M-iQ GreenCoach (optional)
The machine actively detects gaps between items of washware on the conveyor belt. The system responds by only activating the rinse function for the specific sections of belt where dishes have been detected – and the machine can do this while monitoring up to three lanes simultaneously. Reduced fresh water and rinse aid consumption of up to 50% is possible.

Self-cleaning feature
After a long day of warewashing, the M-iQ simply cleans itself with the tank water already in the system. The machine flushes the dirt through the M-iQ filter with just a single fill of the pump final rinse. This continues at periodic intervals until the machine is empty.
Now, only the areas marked in blue still need to be cleaned by hand. With the machine having already done so much of the job automatically, the manual part of the cleaning process can be done more conveniently and thoroughly, and – crucially – quickly and reliably.
M-iQ GreenFilter (optional)
This additional water filtration process in the pump final rinse zone actively removes even the smallest dirt particles.
The process reduces the use of fresh water, delivering potential savings of 10%. And it only deploys detergent and rinse aid where they are actually needed.
CC Touch
The built-in system diagnostics feature keeps users up-to-date at all times. A dynamic progress bar and status indicators clearly show when all the machine’s functions are ready for action.
The display communicates key process data and draws the user’s attention to any deviations or discrepancies.
Even while running, the M-iQ is continuously checking all its systems and functions to ensure hygiene and reliability.
Synergies
M-iQ machines with specialist GreenEye technology® (optional) create new human-machine synergy. By making warewashing more dynamic and more transparent, they continuously optimize the warewashing process. The result is an unparalleled level of efficiency and optimum capacity utilization.
The watchful eye of M-iQ GreenEye technology® has got everything under control. This is your green light for a cleaner and more efficient future!
