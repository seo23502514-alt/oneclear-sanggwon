About the Product and Brand
Panasonic NE-1853 Heavy Duty Microwave
The Panasonic NE-1853 is a simple to use medium-to-high powered commercial microwave capable of a maximum power output of 1800 watts with a robust, stackable stainless-steel body and cavity.
The NE-1853 comes with a good range of features including touch controls, 20 pre-set cook programmes, 3 power levels, double quantity function, and 2 and 3 stage cooking for more sensitive foods.Now comes with a 2-year warranty.
Whilst you are probably aware of the Panasonic brand, what you probably didnʼt know is that Panasonic have been supplying commercial kitchens with products for over 50 years. We have been supplying international customers with a wide range of Commercial Food Service products, specifically designed to meet the needs of the Hospitality industry.
