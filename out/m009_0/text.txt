Product Specs
The Sanremo X-One Grinder is an innovative powerhouse that seamlessly replaces your regular, decaf, and single-dose grinders, making it an essential addition to any bar, restaurant, café, or bakery.
With the ability to grind anything from espresso to French press, the X-One is designed for versatility and efficiency. Its dual hoppers are equipped with industrial-grade weighing systems, allowing for precise dosing from 5-30g per hopper, keeping two different coffees ready to go with tailored recipes for each. Need a quick single dose? No problem! The convenient front bypass allows you to add coffee directly and grind it instantly through the burrs.
Features
3-in-1 versatility
:
Replace your regular, decaf, and single-dose grinders with one powerful machine.
Precision Grinding:
Enjoy single-dose grinding by weight, with the ability to switch between espresso and filter coffee effortlessly.
Dual hoppers with industrial weighing
:
Each hopper doses between 5-30g with separate recipes for different coffees.
Space-saving design
:
Replaces the need for multiple grinders.
Zero-waste grinding
:
An empty grind chamber prevents coffee oxidation, preserving freshness and avoiding rancid flavors.
98mm vertical burrs with Diamond-Like Carbon (DLC) coating
:
Reduces friction, increases lifespan, and improves performance.
Patented weight-by-beans technology
:
Weigh beans before grinding for precise dosing.
Touch display control
:
Choose from 3 standard grinding speeds (1000, 1200, 1400 rpm) or customize between 800-1400 rpm.
Save up to 12 custom recipes
:
Adjustable burr distance, grind speed, and weight settings saved electronically and uploadable via USB.
Pre-dosing feature
:
Automatically refills doses to improve workflow productivity.
Manual dosing buttons
:
One for each hopper.
Bag-friendly design
:
Removable portafilter holder for bag grinding.
BPA-free hoppers and bypass components
:
Safe for food contact.
Burr cooling system
:
Ensures efficiency and longevity.
The Sanremo X-One Grinder combines innovation and functionality in a space-saving design that enhances your coffee experience while reducing clutter. Upgrade your grinding game with the X-One, where versatility meets precision!
About the Brand
Sanremo
is a brand that has set the bar in the industry with coffee machine designs well polished in the style category that are loaded with impressive technology features. The real-time digital displays on some models were inspired by fighter jets and motorcycles and allow the barista to monitor and make changes to water volume, temperature, and shot times giving them more control over the quality of their drinks. Most machines also feature an energy savings system, an eco mode that the machines kick into during idle moments and can save owners 20% in electricity costs making them eco-friendly espresso machines. Sanremo offers premium commercial espresso machines that are built with a passion for professional baristas who love the day-to-day art of coffee and business owners who are looking for custom machines to match their café’s brand and decor. Give your café a cool look and up the vibe with a Sanremo.
