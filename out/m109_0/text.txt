Description
The 1Zpresso JX-Pro coffee grinder is suitable for both Pour-over and Espresso. The fine adjustment allows you to find your “sweet spot” for espresso easily and employs the same burr as the JX grinder which makes a balanced filter of coffee.
Equipped with the same burr with
JX grinder
,
moreover, internal fixing between the burr and central shaft means no more burr wobble or parts that come loose when grinding, making grinding efficient and easy for a more consistent grind. The JX-Pro offers grinding adjustment in the top bean hopper as opposed to bottom adjustment on the 1Zpresso JX grinder.
The body is made of aluminum alloy and equipped with an anti-slip silicone handle. The special 48mm stainless steel extra-large cutter-head ensures a quick and easy grinding process. The crank knob on top prevents jumping beans.
48mm conical burr
48mm conical burr makes greater grinding efficiency. Internal fixing between the burr and central shaft, no more wobbly burr comes loose. The burr was calibrated and fixed in place by the 1Zpresso team, to ensure no wobbly parts or burrs that come loose as time goes on. Estimated grind approx 100-200KG by general use
Fine Adjustment Dial Design
The 1Zpresso JX-Pro coffee grinder is equipped with top adjustment with 10 numbers / 40 clicks per rotation, 12.5 microns each click. The central shaft and burr are fixed together, enhancing grinding consistency. The grinder has over 40 clicks of filter grind range, ideal for your favorite pour-over with full-bodied flavor. Also, if you would like to drink espresso occasionally, there are 20 clicks within the espresso range
Assembly Design
1Zpresso JX-Pro grinders are able to dismantle without any tool and allow thorough cleaning and maintenance without re-calibrating the burr
1Zpresso JX-Pro Grind Setting Reference
Attention
Do not clean the grinder with water. The grinder should only ever be cleaned with a dry brush and blower.
Please read the instruction manual and grind setting reference guide before use.
The burr was calibrated and fixed in place by the 1Zpresso team. Disassembly of the burr may affect grind accuracy and cause damage to the grinder.
