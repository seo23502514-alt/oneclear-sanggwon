BAKERTOP-X™: the most innovative combi oven designed to bake perfection.
Smart professional high-performance
electric combi oven
designed to perfectly handle any baking process. Nevertheless, it is also ideal for gastronomy cooking processes. Its cutting-edge operating system and its technological features allow to optimize and simplify every baker’s routine. With its 600x400 trays, it adapts to all types of conditions and businesses. BAKERTOP-X
™
is available in the 5-tray or 10-tray versions.
Why is BAKERTOP-X
™
the perfect solution for bakery professionals?
Low-consumption baking performance
: BAKERTOP-X™ combi oven is
ENERGY STAR
®
certified. Thanks to advanced technologies such as SMART.Energy, it allows you to monitor water and energy consumption and optimise each baking process with practical energy-saving tips.
High level of baking uniformity and repeatability
: humidity levels are always under control thanks to BAKERTOP-X
™
top-notch technological features, such as AIR.Maxi and STEAM.Maxi. It detects moisture and adapts baking parameters to guarantee flawless results regardless of the food type and load.
Ease of use and
intuitive interactions:
Digital.ID
™
, its ground-breaking operating system, is designed to offer the most incredible user experience. The interface is responsive, immersive and interact with users asking for their feedback and learning from them how to improve its baking performance. The high-end display enables remote assistance and voice control.
Tailored user experience and comforts:
bakers can build their own account based on their preferences placing the widgets of favourite programs on the colorful home page. Moreover, they can access the oven from their different devices to control it remotely through the Digital.ID
™
app. A new way to live and work in bakeries.
Top-notch materials and extreme reliability
: its 316L steel structure has been designed to endure the wear and tear of time and usage. It is equipped with an integrated RO.Care water filtering system to reduce to zero limescale formation and eliminate the need for maintenance.
