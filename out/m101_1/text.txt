Overview
La Radiosa was engineered to answer every need in terms of efficiency, flexibility and functionality. With its 21 g espresso brew group and its simultaneous dispensing of coffee and steam, you’ll surely excel. Don’t worry about security and sanitary concerns; La Radiosa Cool Touch, non-sticky wand and complete cleaning management features will make it safe for everyone. Finally, La Radiosa can become your own: choose the content of the 2 whole bean and one soluble ingredient canisters, and fully customize the 10″ touch screen.
La Radiosa is the superautomatic coffee extravaganza that will leave everybody speechless. Live up to your coffee expectations and let La Radiosa run the show.
