Description
The CBS-1252 Plus Series Twin Station Coffee Brewer provides flexibility in medium to large-sized venues such as
        cafeterias, restaurants, specialty cafes, and banquet halls. With the easy-to-use push button interface, brewing
        great coffee has never been more simple. The CBS-1252 Plus is designed with high-volume batch brewing, but still
        possess the reliability and many of the phenomenal features offered by larger, Extractor®-branded products from
        Fetco. The Cascading Dome Spray (CDS) found on the CBS-1252 Plus virtually eliminates spray head lime and
        mineral buildup as the water flows over the outer surface of the dome as opposed to through tiny drain holes
        found on many other batch brewers. New features introduced in the 1200 series include an electrical lattice,
        front panel access for servicing, laser welded tanks for better durability, and a USB port for software updates.
        This brewer comes standard with a highly durable plastic basket for optimal heat retention and cost savings, but
        these can also be upgraded to
stainless steel
