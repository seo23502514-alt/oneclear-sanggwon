Not only is the Panasonic NE-1853 Commercial Microwave designed for performance, and efficiency but also for ease of use. Ideal for busy, demanding kitchens such as pubs, restaurants, and many more. As well as dual emission top and bottom energy feed ensuring even cooking of your food.
As well as that it offers focus heating from above, below, or both. This results in food being cooked safely and effectively. Not to mention the boost button giving the food that extra 10-20 seconds when it is needed. Additionally, the clear LCD display allows you to monitor power levels, time and self-diagnostic codes at a glance and there is an LED interior light that can be programmed to flash at the end of the cooking cycle.
Offering 30 programs to cover all your food re-heating needs, including 2 and 3 stage heating, automatically changing the power level. The programmable options can be used for x2 and x3 quantity heating, plus it comes with a program lock for a one-touch operation, especially helpful when you have the same time and power cooking instructions for certain foods and there is even a repeat function.
Also, the Panasonic NE1853 microwave also provides the opportunity to save space. As the vents are not on the top of bottom, two microwave ovens can be securely stacked vertically, reducing space needed for multiple microwaves.
Finally, the stainless-steel body and cavity and removable ceiling plate allow for an easy to clean interior. The removable air filter will also alert you when it's time to change the filter and the cycle counter records the number of operations so you can track how often it is used between deep cleans. If you are looking for further benefits of cleaning then the
CP2SA Microsave cavity liner
, which is designed purposely for the NE1853 model, is the perfect addition to not only increase the longevity of your microwave oven but minimise cleaning time between uses.
Panasonic NE-1853 Commercial Microwave Features:
• Programmable touch pads, 15 power levels.
• Stainless steel body & cavity.
• Easy to clean interior with removable ceiling plate.
• Dual emission top & bottom energy feed ensures even heating.
• Clear LCD display of power levels, time & self diagnostic codes.
• Removable air filter for easy cleaning.
• Two ovens can be securely stacked one on top of another.
• LED Interior light can be programmed to flash at the end of the cooking cycle.
• 10/20 second boost button. Just a single press provides an additional 10/20 seconds if needed.
• Self diagnostic.
• 30 programs to include 2 and 3 stage heating.
• Programmed option for x2 and x3 quantity heating.
• Program lock to allow one touch operation.
• Repeat function (for manual heating only).
• Clean air filter reminder.
• Cycle counter – records the number of operations.
• Beep tone option (can be silenced).
• Pull open door with wide easy view window.
