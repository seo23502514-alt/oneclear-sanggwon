Product Description
Bezzera Domus Galatea
The Domus Galatea is the classic, iconic Bezzera model which is timeless in both appearance and performance.
It boasts a generous 2L Heat Exchange boiler which allows for both brewing espresso and simultaneous, unlimited milk steaming.
The unique design of the piping ensures the machine extracts perfect shot after shot.
The retro styling makes the Domus Galatea a crowed favourite which stands out in any home environment.
