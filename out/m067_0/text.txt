El Rocio Espresso Machine - Manus S White
$1,500.00
Overview
The El Rocio Manus S is a compact espresso machine, hand-built in Korea, for home baristas who want real control without a big machine on the counter. It brews and steams at the same time, lets you set the pre-infusion and shape each shot with a flow-control dial, and its eco mode cuts power use between coffees. This one is in white.
Good to know
From cold, the Manus S takes about 20 minutes to heat up fully.
Our take
El Rocio is still little known in Europe, but the Manus S gives you a lot of control and solid build quality for the price. Buy it from Bean Bros and you get a 2-year warranty with service in Europe.
Features
01
Hand-built in Korea
Each Manus S is hand-built in Korea, with careful attention to detail and long-lasting durability.
Its sleek finish suits a modern kitchen and brings professional-level performance to your countertop.
02
Simple dials, easy brewing
A user-friendly dial system makes it easy for beginners and seasoned baristas alike to pull a great espresso.
Temperature, pre-infusion time and eco mode are all set from the same dials, so there is no menu to learn.
03
Shape every shot
Set the pre-infusion time to match your taste, from a quick extraction to a deeper, richer cup.
Flow rate control lets you set the pressure with the dial, so you stay in control whether you brew a light or a dark roast, with consistent results shot after shot.

Frequently asked questions
What is the pre-infusion time feature in El Rocio Manu S?
The pre-infusion time feature allows you to set a pre-infusion period between 1-10 seconds, ensuring the machine does not exceed 2 bars of pressure. This helps in achieving a more balanced and flavorful espresso shot.
How does the eco mode in EL Rocio Manu S save energy?
The eco mode reduces electricity consumption by up to 39%. After a set period, the machine enters eco mode and only requires 5 minutes to heat back up to the optimal brewing temperature of 93 degrees Celsius.
How quickly does the El Rocio Manus S heat up?
The Manus S heats up fully in just 20 minutes, allowing you to brew espresso quickly and efficiently.
Can I adjust the flow rate manually?
Yes, the EL Rocio Manus S features a flow rate control dial, enabling you to set the pressure manually for a fully customized extraction process.
Is the El Rocio Manus S easy to maintain?
Absolutely. The machine includes an effortless drainage system with a valve by the drip tray, making it easy to drain the boiler when not in use for extended periods or during transportation.
Can I brew coffee and steam milk simultaneously on the EL Rocio Manu S?
Yes, the EL Rocio Manus S is designed with an extraction tube inside the steam boiler, allowing you to brew coffee and steam milk at the same time, enhancing your efficiency.
Are there any additional fees for international shipments?
International shipments may be subject to additional fees and customs processing. The recipient is responsible for any import duties and taxes imposed by the destination country. It is important to understand these potential charges before completing a purchase.
