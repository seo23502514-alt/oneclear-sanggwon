On-demand
The grinder that designs flavor
The ideal grinder to enhance
flavor clarity
, making it perfect for specialty coffee enthusiasts.
Ground temperature stability.
Weight precision.
Granulometry consistency.
Zero Retention.
RSZ83
83 mm Red Speed coated burrs with lifespan of 4 tons.
Sweep out dual spin
Designed to preserve volatile compounds, the
on-demand coffee grinder Rev Zero
enhances the aromatic complexity in the cup thanks to reduced retention,
just ~2g between the burrs
, which prevents alterations in temperature, gas content, and flavor.
It delivers 99% of the ground coffee
directly into the portafilter, ensuring a clean flow, consistent performance, and
reducing waste by up to 5 doses per adjustment
.
GTM, WAM, and Steady Lock
technologies work in synergy to ensure precise dosing and uniform grind size under all operating conditions.
Weight adjustment module
Consistent weight aids in building and monitoring flavour recipes.
The REV Series feature an
interchangeable weight module
that can be mounted in under two minutes, even post grinder installation.
Steady Lock
The grinder features a counteracting system that
expands in the opposite direction
of the burrs, neutralizing the effect of heat expansion. This ensures a consistent particle size at any pace, preserving the same flavor profile.

Cleaning has never been easier
Only 3 screws
Burrs are easy to clean and inspect as it takes only 3 screws to remove the top of the grind chamber. No grind re-adjustment is needed after cleaning.

cleaning reminder
periodically appears on display.
