About K-UP
Elektra K-UP’s minimalist styling and sleek design make it a popular choice where a more modern look is required. The large work surface and tall groups (13.5 cm to bottom of coffee spout) allow enough clearance for 16oz cup sizes. Two risers are included if you prefer to use smaller cups on one or both groups.
Patented steam joysticks that lock open in any direction with steam tips ideal for micro-foaming, and programmable back-flushing allow for ease of use and consistency. The K-UP's unique system of grouping the two steam wands together on the left side, while the brew groups are on the right, allows two baristas to operate side by side without getting in each other's way, increasing efficiency during busy periods.
Steam pressure can also be adjusted for different coffee profiles. Standard Volumetric programming allows the barista to program 4 espresso shot volumes per group, allowing for much more consistent volume every time. Shots are not only more consistent between different users, but there is also more time for the barista to interact with customers, or begin another order as there is no need to monitor the espresso as carefully.
Version Details
2-Group K-Up
Features 2 brew groups, 2 steam wands, 1 hot water spout, and a large 12 litre boiler for excellent steam & hot water capacity.
Recommended for:
medium volume locations, coffee shops, restaurants and hotels or locations with less experienced baristas
3-Group K-Up
Features 3 brew groups, 2 steam wands, 1 hot water spout, and a large 16 litre boiler for maximum performance.
Recommended for:
medium to higher volume locations, coffee shops, restaurants and hotels or locations with less experienced baristas
Features
Body panels can be removed using 4 allen-key screws (with cup-tray remaining in place) for easy servicing and customization
Full painted steel body on a solid steel frame
Volumetric Dosing means the Barista can program 4 espresso shot volumes (single/double/short/long) per group (identically or independently) for one-touch brewing operation & consistency between users
LCD Display allows Barista to control features: boiler pressure temperature (limited range), sleep mode, maintenance features, backflushing, self-diagnostics and service alarms
Pivoting “joystick” instant-on steam control avoids stress on Barista’s hand – press gently in any direction for purging or lock it for continuous steaming
Ergonomic work space has coffee area on the right and steam/hot water on the left of a large open drip tray area for optimum efficiency
Raised groups fit up to 16oz cups, with extra risers included for smaller cups
Large boiler & fast heat recovery means most sustained steam pressure and simultaneous tea/americano water
5 lb brass groups provide improved brew temperature stability
Portafilters are solid chrome-plated brass with ergonomic maple-wood grips that minimize stress on Barista’s wrists
Programmable Backflushing for fast and easy group cleaning
Non-slip feet are also height-adjustable
