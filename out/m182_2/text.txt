The Samsung CM1919 is a powerful, 1850w, manual dial control microwave oven, ideal for a variety of busy commercial catering businesses. Manufactured with a stainless steel exterior and interior and a 2/3 gastronorm static ceramic base, the CM1919 is built to withstand the day-to-day rigours of the kitchen whether you are cooking, defrosting or reheating. With a 26 litre capacity, this Samsung microwave features 5 power settings and digital display.
