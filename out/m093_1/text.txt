Product Details
Key Features
Automated Coffee Dosing: This espresso machine features an electronic system for setting automatic doses, ensuring consistent quality and taste in every cup.
Advanced Temperature Management: Electronic boiler temperature regulation, coupled with a LED display for temperature indication, ensures precise control for optimum coffee extraction.
Dual Pressure Control: Equipped with double pressure gauges, this machine allows for meticulous boiler and pump pressure monitoring.
Efficient Heating System: The electric heating system includes options for an upgraded boiler heating element, enhancing performance and reliability.
Versatility in Service: Features a hot water outlet and two steam wands for diverse coffee creations, and grids for espresso cups enhance functionality.
Convenience Features: Includes an electric cup warmer and the capability for preset hot water delivery, ideal for tea and other infusions.
Eco-Friendly Options: The ECO MODE function reduces energy consumption without compromising functionality.
Enhanced Accessibility: A 16 cm clearance between the delivery spouts and the drip tray accommodates larger “take away” cups.
Integrated Volumetric Pump: Ensures consistent coffee volume and optimal extraction.
Alarm System: Monitors and alerts for any malfunctions to maintain smooth operation.
Customizable Options: Users can request additional features like an electric cup warmer or an upgraded boiler heating element to suit specific needs.
Dimensions & Specs
Dimensions: 1000x530x600 mm (LxWxH)
Weight: 80 kg
Boiler Capacity: 15 liters
Power Requirements: Available in 220/240/400 volts, with a frequency of 50/60 Hz and a wattage range of 4300-6300.
Water Filtration Requirements
Protect your investment and guarantee the best taste with our recommended water filtration systems:
For Single Boiler Machines: BWT Single Cartridge Filter enhances flavor and prevents scale.
For Double Boiler Machines: Optipure Reverse Osmosis Water System offers maximum purity and protective benefits, perfect for any high-demand setting.
Cleaning & Maintenance
Daily Cleaning
Wipe and purge the steam wand after each use to prevent milk buildup, rinse portafilters and filter baskets, clean the drip tray, and backflush the group head to remove coffee oils and residue.
Weekly Maintenance
Soak portafilters, baskets, and shower screens in an approved cleaning solution, inspect group head gaskets and screens, and perform a deeper backflush to keep internal components clean.
Long-Term Care
Maintain proper water filtration to prevent scale buildup and descale the machine as needed based on water quality and usage to ensure long-lasting performance and consistent espresso quality.
Plumbing Requirements
For this
single-boiler espresso machine
, we recommend using a
BWT Single Cartridge Water Filter
or a comparable inline filtration system designed to reduce hardness, chlorine, and sediment. Regular filter replacement based on water usage and local water quality is strongly advised to extend the life of the machine and preserve flavor clarity.
Power Requirements
This machine requires a 208-240V power supply, which calls for commercial-grade electrical infrastructure rather than a standard outlet — plan for a dedicated 208-240V circuit and have a licensed electrician confirm the installation. It draws 4300-6300 watts and is built for fixed commercial locations rather than mobile setups.
