Perfect Moose
Introducing the Perfect
Moose
– a barista’s best friend and extra pair of hands…
Are you looking to improve the workflow of your café or espresso bar without compromising on speed or consistency? If the answer is yes then you should meet ‘Greg’ and ‘Jack’
‘Greg’ and ‘Jack’ are the 2 options available in the auto milk frother range. Firstly, both models achieve the same thing – consistent and perfectly textured milk for all types of milk. Yes that’s correct, ALL MILK TYPES, not just one milk. Secondly, the smart moose milk pitcher has a chip on the bottom which triggers the unit to start steaming.
Your customers will certainly enjoy the same great quality coffee being consistently served no matter how many different Barista’s are working.  This is simply industry leading technology and unifying your products.
‘Greg’ is designed to be connected to your coffee machine. This means it will operate from the mains water and steam boiler supply from your coffee machine. It would be recommended to have a larger coffee machine and/or a multi boiler to ensure supply is adequate. The best place for ‘Greg’ would be next to the coffee machine.
‘Jack’ is designed to be a stand alone auto milk steamer. This requires connecting the unit to mains water (filtered water). This means you can position ‘Jack’ wherever you like however, next to your jug rinser would be ideal.
