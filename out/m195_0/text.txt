About
Description
FELICITA INCLINE
Engineered for precision and built to last, the
Felicita Incline
scale is the perfect companion for serious brewers. With its
high sensitivity
,
fast response time
, and
durable aluminum body
, it delivers consistent performance for every pour.
The Incline supports
auto-tare and auto-timer functions
, making it effortless to track your brew. It’s also compatible with the
Felicita app
for iOS and Android, allowing you to log and refine your brewing process like a pro.
Key Features
2000g capacity
with
0.1g precision
Auto-tare and auto-timer
modes for seamless brewing
Bluetooth connectivity
with the Felicita app (iOS/Android)
Customizable smart auto-off
function
Fast and stable response
for high-accuracy brewing
Compact design
with a durable aluminum body
Specifications
Capacity
: 2000g
Precision
: 0.1g increments
Dimensions
: 130 x 160 x 26 mm
Functions
: Auto-tare, Auto-timer, App compatible, Smart auto-off
