Quamar Q13/T Electronic Instant Coffee Grinder
Quamar has developed the Q13 Touch, an instant coffee grinder from the new TOUCH series.
The Q13/T is the result of numerous studies to improve the performance of the classic electronic bottled Q13 models. This research to improve the performance of classic models has led to the creation of a renewed, efficient product with a modern design, while maintaining the Quamar style.
The main feature that distinguishes the Touch series is the presence of the new display with touch keyboard. The user interface is available in seven languages.
The Q13 Touch coffee grinder can be equipped with a variety of optional accessories, such as, for example, coffee collection plate, bell with different capacities (150 g – 250 g – 500 g – 1200 g – 1500 g), plug in Australia. UK variants, USA, Brazil, Switzerland and Israel.
The short fork is the standard for the Q13/Touch; its grinding speed means you do not need to remove the handle from the filter holder and guarantees perfect grinding in a short time. The fork with non-slip rubber tip can also be long and allows the filter holder to be supported without manual support from the operator.
Available in eight colors: graphite black, matte black, gloss black, gloss black, gloss aluminum, silver gray, gloss white, gloss orange, gloss red.
Screen with touch keyboard
Using the keyboard it is possible to easily select the dose of coffee to be ground: single, double or manual. The dose time setting is highly intuitive and with just a few taps it is possible to change the grinding seconds for each dose. 16-character 2-line display with black background and white LEDs Touch keyboard with active area dimensions 56.20×11.5
