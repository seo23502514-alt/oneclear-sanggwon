CIME CO-05 PID
Send an enquiry
The CO-05 is the harmonious set of reliable and robust elements that make up the beating heart of an espresso machine, where functionality and design give value to the art of knowing how to prepare excellent coffee.
This is a great coffee machine, perfect for a domestic usage, which has a variety of extraordinary features including a E61 group head, commercial calibre parts, and a copper boiler. In addition, the entire body is handmade with stainless steel in Italy. In two different versions using a vibrating or rotary pump. The rotary pump version needs to be connected into the water supply. You are able to brew a cup of black coffee while at the same time use the steam wand. It has a thermosiphon system for temperature stability. A computer chip checks and refills the boiler with water as needed. It has a single scale gauge to indicate pressure, as well as steam and hot water jets.
GENERAL FEATURES
Thermosiphon circulation.
Electronic infusion.
Automatic group washing.
Hot water and coffee dose adjustments.
Warning light for lack of water in the boiler.
Boiler water drain cock.
Groups dispensing emergency buttons.
Side solenoid valve.
Lower dinghy with hydraulic infuser.
