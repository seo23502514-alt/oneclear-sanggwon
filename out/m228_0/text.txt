Why choose the Übermilk ONE Dairy · Hot?
The Übermilk ONE Dairy · Hot is the original automatic milk foamer: manual aeration, hot microfoam on demand, and dosing you can program to the millilitre. It is the right choice for dairy-led bars that want speed, consistency and a simpler workflow without changing how the drinks taste.
Need cold foam or touchscreen foam control? See the ONE Dairy · Hot & Cold. Mostly oat and plant milks? See the ONE Plant-Based.
Includes a 1-month Starter Kit.
30 × Filter Discs A4001 (ONE Dairy)
2 × 1L Detergent No. 1 (alkaline)
1 × 1L Detergent No. 2 (acid)
1 × Lined measuring jug
Key features.
Hot microfoam on demand:
velvety microfoam at the touch of a button, with the volume, temperature and texture you set.
Manual aeration:
foam level is set on the aeration valve, so every barista pours the same foam.
Adjustable temperature:
55–68°C (62–65°C recommended), held to ±0.5°C.
Programmable dosing:
pre-set quantities per drink size to cut milk waste and protect food cost.
Stainless-steel build:
food-safe stainless casing and heating elements. Available in INOX, Black and White.
Dairy milk:
uses A4001 filter discs. For plant milks see the ONE Plant-Based.

Operational benefits.
Maximise Throughput:
Increase coffee volumes by streamlining the milk foaming process, reducing barista idle time and enabling faster drink preparation.
Minimise Milk Waste:
Precise, programmable dosing minimises milk waste, directly lowering food costs and improving profitability.
Reduce Labour Costs and Training:
Automates the milk texturing process, reducing the need for extensive barista training. Frees up staff for other value-added tasks.
Maintain Consistent Quality, Reduce Variability:
Ensures every drink meets your quality standards, enhancing customer satisfaction and loyalty. Removes the variations inherent in manual milk texturing.
Simplified Workflow:
Integrates seamlessly into existing workflows, improving overall efficiency in high-volume environments.
